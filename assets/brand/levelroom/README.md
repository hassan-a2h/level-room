# Levelroom brand kit

These assets give the app a consistent identity across GitHub, documentation, and launch material.

## Files

- `levelroom-mark.png` — transparent portal-and-trail mark for avatars, favicons, and app chrome.
- `levelroom-wordmark.svg` — light-background wordmark with the recommended tagline.
- `levelroom-banner-dark.png` — dark GitHub/README hero background.
- `levelroom-banner-light.png` — light-mode GitHub/README hero background.
- `levelroom-social-preview.svg` — ready-made dark social preview with title and product positioning.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest ink | Primary dark | `#17211C` |
| Trail green | Primary action | `#337C55` |
| Checkpoint blue | Secondary action | `#315FDA` |
| Warm coral | Feedback accent | `#F28A78` |
| Milestone sun | Progress highlight | `#F2C65B` |
| Paper | Light canvas | `#FAFAF8` |

Keep the wordmark on quiet space. Use the dark banner with light copy and the light banner with dark copy; do not place small text over the detailed trail area.
