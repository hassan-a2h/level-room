## Shared fidelity gates

- Keep required instructional wording, question/answer text, outcomes, chapter names, errors, actions and mastery information identical across packs.
- Maintain semantic heading order, visible focus, readable contrast, keyboard operation and reduced-motion behavior.
- Do not encode state through color alone. Maintain the board’s character without sacrificing control size or readable line length.
- At narrow widths, reflow content in its reading order; no clipped controls, nested horizontal scroll, or required content hidden behind decorative art.
- Use production artwork designed for its view. Board images remain documentation only.
